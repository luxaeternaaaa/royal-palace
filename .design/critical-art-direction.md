## Judgment

The current site is coherent and substantially more careful than a generic first draft. The actual venue, authentic logo, restrained green/champagne identity and clear type hierarchy work together. Calling the whole result mediocre would overstate the evidence. The remaining template impression comes mainly from the ratio of generic advice to venue-specific information, and from repeatedly showing the same celebration rather than helping someone understand the spaces. Color, serif type, an ordered three-step process or subtle fades alone are not evidence of AI authorship.

No new high-severity visual/access blocker was demonstrated in this pass. The following are medium-priority content/decision improvements and lower-priority editorial refinements. The separate reliability review remains necessary for complete flow coverage.

## Verified strengths and non-findings

- No horizontal overflow at1440/390/320; no page errors during this batch. Full-page heights were6,263 /7,918 /8,526px. Length by itself is not a bug; A2/A3 identify specific low-value contributors.
- The hero establishes category, city, three halls and an immediate next action. Its text remains readable in the inspected crops; the primary button and secondary contact are distinguishable. Keep its current hierarchy.
- The three-hall capacities are correct; no fabricated number appears for the small hall.
- The menu's demo disclaimer is visible, not hidden. It is not deceptive merely because approved menu data is missing.
- New typography is actually loaded, not merely named in CSS: CDP `CSS.getPlatformFontsForNode` reports custom Forum for h1/brand and custom Golos Text for hero body. Separate probes for **ӘәҒғҚқҢңӨөҰұҮүҺһІі** return all18glyphs from Forum and Golos respectively, no fallback. The heading/body division is appropriate; replacing these again would not address A1–A6.
- Do not infer a sticky-CTA regression from a screenshot taken immediately after scripted scrolling: observer callbacks and CSS transitions can lag capture. No such bug is claimed here.
